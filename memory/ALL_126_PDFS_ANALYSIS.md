## TAX BENEFITS FRAMEWORK (From EY Reports)

### Direct Tax Incentives
- **Income Tax Holiday**: 10 consecutive years out of 15
- **MAT (Minimum Alternate Tax)**: Reduced rate of 9% (inapplicable under new tax regime)
- **Dividend Withholding**: Concessional 10% on non-resident payments
- **Specified Fund Status**: Available for eligible AIFs

### Indirect Tax
- **Customs Duty**: Reduced/exempted on approved imports
- **GST**: Simplified compliance

### Eligibility
- Must be registered entity with IFSCA
- Derive income from IFSC business
- Subject to compliance with IFSCA regulations
