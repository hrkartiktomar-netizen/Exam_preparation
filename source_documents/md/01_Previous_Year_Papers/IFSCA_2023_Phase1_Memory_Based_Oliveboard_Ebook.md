# IFSCA Grade A Phase 1 Memory Based Questions

1. Which of the following SDG is related to Gender Inequality?
Answer: Goal 5 (Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls)

2. According to National Education Policy, instruction in Home language are provided from
Answer: Grade 5 to Grade 8

3. K K Birla Foundation gives Saraswati Samman. Which of the following are other awards given by KK Birla Foundation?
Answer: Vyas Samman

4. As per census, ___________________ age & above who can read & write are called literate
Answer: 7 years

5. What is the score for the states/UTs under the Front Runner category in the SDG Index?
Answer: 65 - 99 (100 is achiever)

6. Which of the following are ineligible under PM-KISAN scheme
Answer: Farmers who have 10 ha land

7. David Malpas has announced his resignation in Feb 2023. He is the head of which of the following organizations?
Answer: World Bank

8. Recently, the IMF downgraded world growth from 3.4% to 2.9%. Which of the following was the reason for it?
Answer:
1. Central Bank of developed countries increasing rates to control inflation.
2. War in Ukraine

9. There is a person whose age is 29 years and he wants to get 3000 as monthly pension under PM - SYM. How much amount does he have to contribute every month and by what age he will start getting pension every month?
Answer: Rs 100, 60 years

10. Recently PM Modi inaugurated Hubli Railway Stations. It comes under which railway zone?
Answer: South Western Railway Zone

# IFSCA Grade A Phase 1 Memory Based Questions

11. The G20 2023 troika consists of which of the following countries apart from India?
Answer: Indonesia and Brazil

12. Which of the following is the domain of Population study?
Answer: Migration, Birth, Death

13. As per NFHS - 5, there are five total states which have TFR rate 2.1 or above. Which of the following states is not one of them?
Answer: There are **only five states in India** which are **above replacement level of fertility of 2.1**. These states are **Bihar, Meghalaya, Uttar Pradesh, Jharkhand and Manipur** so correct answer is MP

14. As per Education Policy 2020, the central government is aiming to achieve_______________gross enrollment rate (GER) in higher education by ________________.
Answer: 50%, 2035

15. NASA's labelled LUS 475b planet is of similar size as which of the following planets?
Answer: Earth

16. Inventories are valued at the lower of cost or the net realisable value. This accounting principle is known as ______________.
Answer: Prudence Concept of Accounting

17. What is Working Capital?
Answer: It is excess of current assets over current liabilities.

18. Advertisement expenses are treated as_________________.
Answer: Selling Overhead

19. Important goal of Financial Management is____________________.
Answer: Wealth Maximization

20. NABARD refinance financial institutions which are financing ________
Answer: Rural sector

21. Batch Casting is a type of?
Answer: Job Costing

22. Which of the following does not depreciate?
Answer: Land

23. A subsidy of Rs 80,000 from the government for working capital by a manufacturing concern will come in____________.
Answer: Revenue Receipt
