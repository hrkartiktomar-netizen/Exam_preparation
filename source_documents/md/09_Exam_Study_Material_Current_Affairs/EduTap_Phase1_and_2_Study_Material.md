• For Quant, Reasoning & English Live classes are in Hinglish.

9 Can I use this course on mobile as well as on a laptop?
Yes, we believe in "Learn Anytime, Anywhere." You can access this course through an Android
based mobile or tab by downloading an EduTap app from the Playstore and logging in. In
laptop/desktop, you can just login to the portal and access your course.

10 How can I contact/talk to faculty if I have some doubts?
We do not believe in "sell and forget" sort of thing. We have a dedicated support team and
faculty to answer all your queries promptly. We have established 3 channels of communication
between the student and faculty:
1. A dedicated discussion forum - the student can post a query on the subject wise discussion
forum, the concerned faculty regularly checks the discussion forum for queries and replies
2. A dedicated mail address - students can drop a mail on **hello@edutap.co.in** mentioning the
subject name or the name of the faculty they wish to talk to. Upon receipt of this mail, the
faculty is connected to the student on their registered phone number.
3. A dedicated helpline number - students can call us on **8146207241** (between 9 am to 6 pm
on working days) and ask for a strategy cum mentorship call with the faculty.

11 Can I use this course on the iPhone/iPad?
