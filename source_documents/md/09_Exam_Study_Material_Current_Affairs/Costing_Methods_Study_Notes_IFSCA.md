## Part A: RBI Grade B Finance & Management - Costing-Related Topics

RBI Grade B does **NOT have a dedicated costing/cost accounting syllabus** in Phase 2. The Finance & Management paper (50 marks objective + 50 marks descriptive) covers only **"Basics of Accounting and Financial Statements"** — specifically ratio analysis, balance sheet understanding, and P&L interpretation.
