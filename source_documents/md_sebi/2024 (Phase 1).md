# Section – General English

**Direction (1-7): Read the passage carefully then answer the questions given below.**

The presence of humans on Earth has significantly transformed the natural environment in numerous and complex ways. From the rapid development of sprawling urban areas to extensive deforestation, human activities have exerted profound and often detrimental impacts on various ecosystems. The advent of industrialization, marked by the extensive use of machinery and energy resources, has led to the widespread alteration of landscapes and a notable loss of natural habitats for countless species. This transformation is evident in the loss of forests, wetlands, and grasslands, which were once teeming with diverse flora and fauna.

Urban expansion has further exacerbated environmental degradation. As cities grow, they encroach upon previously undisturbed areas, fragmenting natural habitats and reducing the available space for wildlife. This encroachment not only contributes to habitat loss but also increases pollution levels, as the demand for resources and waste production rise. Air pollution, resulting from industrial emissions and vehicular exhaust, has severely impacted air quality, leading to health problems for both humans and animals. Similarly, water pollution from industrial runoff, agricultural practices, and improper waste disposal has compromised the quality of water resources, affecting aquatic life and human health.

Climate change, driven primarily by the emission of greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane, compounds these environmental challenges. The accumulation of these gases in the atmosphere leads to global warming, which disrupts established weather patterns and increases the frequency and severity of extreme weather events. Hurricanes, floods, droughts, and heatwaves are becoming more common and intense, posing significant risks to ecosystems, agriculture, and human settlements.

Despite technological advancements and a growing awareness of environmental issues, the challenge of reconciling human progress with the need for environmental conservation remains urgent and complex. Efforts to mitigate environmental impacts include promoting sustainable practices, investing in renewable energy sources, and enhancing conservation efforts. However, achieving a balance between development and environmental protection requires ongoing commitment, innovative solutions, and a collaborative approach across various sectors of society.
