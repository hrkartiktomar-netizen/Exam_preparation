**1. How Environment, Social and Governance (ESG) norms help in Global business prospects. Comment.**  
**Model Essay:**

ESG factors represent a company's commitment to sustainable and responsible practices, and adhering to these norms can lead to long-term benefits such as enhanced brand value, risk mitigation, and access to global capital markets. Companies in India that adopt green practices, like reducing carbon emissions and investing in renewable energy, position themselves as environmentally conscious. This alignment with international environmental regulations and consumer preferences boosts their appeal in global markets. India's push towards clean energy, with a target of 500 GW by 2030, encourages businesses to adopt eco-friendly practices, making them attractive to international investors.

Furthermore, focusing on social impact by promoting diversity, community development, and employee well-being enhances a company's reputation. Indian companies with strong Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives are better positioned to collaborate with international organizations that prioritize ethical labor practices and community impact. Such initiatives not only improve brand loyalty but also help in building a positive corporate image that resonates well with global audiences.

Strong governance is equally crucial for building trust with stakeholders. Indian firms that implement robust governance structures attract more foreign direct investment (FDI). A transparent governance framework ensures accountability and ethical conduct, which are key factors for investors assessing the viability of long-term partnerships. In 2022, India witnessed a surge in ESG-compliant investments, with global investors pouring $30.5 billion into companies that demonstrated strong ESG frameworks.

In conclusion, adopting ESG norms helps Indian businesses improve their global reputation, attract international investments, and secure long-term sustainability. This alignment creates a win-win scenario for companies and global stakeholders. As India becomes more integrated into global markets, adherence to ESG principles will be a crucial factor for success, enabling businesses to thrive in an increasingly conscious consumer landscape.

**4. “1 machine can replace 5 average human beings, but 5 machines cannot replace 1 smart human.’ Elaborate.**

**Model Answer:**

The statement “one machine can replace five average human beings, but five machines cannot replace one smart human” underscores the evolving dynamics between technology and human intelligence in the modern workforce. As automation and artificial intelligence continue to advance, the ability of machines to perform repetitive and predictable tasks has increased dramatically. However, this does not imply that machines can replicate the creativity, critical thinking, and emotional intelligence inherent in human beings.

To begin with, machines are designed to execute specific tasks with precision and efficiency. For instance, in manufacturing settings, robots can operate assembly lines at speeds and accuracy levels that far exceed human capabilities. This allows a single machine to effectively replace several average workers who may lack the speed or consistency needed for tasks like assembly, quality control, or data entry. In this context, machines excel in environments where processes are straightforward and do not require complex problem-solving skills.

However, the advent of smart technology raises important questions about the limitations of machines in areas requiring human insight. Five machines working in unison may successfully complete repetitive tasks, but they lack the ability to innovate, strategize, or adapt to unforeseen circumstances. For example, a smart human can analyze data patterns, identify market trends, and devise strategies that respond to consumer needs in a way that machines cannot. In areas such as marketing, management, or research and development, the ability to think critically and creatively remains paramount. A smart human can assess various factors—emotional responses, cultural influences, and ethical considerations—when making decisions, something machines are not equipped to do.

Furthermore, the importance of emotional intelligence cannot be overstated. In fields such as healthcare, education, and customer service, the ability to empathize, communicate effectively, and build relationships is essential. While machines can process vast amounts of information, they cannot replicate the human touch that fosters trust and connection.

Moreover, the rapid evolution of technology creates an environment where adaptability and continuous learning are critical. A smart human can learn new skills, adapt to changing circumstances, and embrace technological advancements to enhance their capabilities. In contrast, machines require constant programming and updates to function effectively. This dynamic means that humans are better suited for roles that involve learning and adaptation, while machines may excel in static environments.

In conclusion, while machines have transformed industries by replacing average human labor, they cannot replicate the unique qualities that smart humans possess. In the future, success will increasingly depend on the ability to harness the strengths of both machines and smart humans, creating a workforce that is not only efficient but also innovative, adaptable, and
