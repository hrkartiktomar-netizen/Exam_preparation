Subject: Recruitment of Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) 2025 – Result of Phase-II 
Examination held on November 15, 2025 
Roll Numbers of the candidates who have been shortlisted for the Interview (General Stream, Legal 
Stream, and Information Technology Stream) for the recruitment to the post of Officer Grade A 
(Assistant Manager) 2025 in IFSCA are Annexed herewith. 
2. 
It may be noted that mere shortlisting of a candidate for interview does not confer any right 
to appointment, nor does it imply that the candidate has been finally selected. The shortlisting is 
purely provisional and subject to Clause VIII(9) of the vacancy notification and to the applicable 
provisions of relevant Acts, Rules, regulations, guidelines, instructions, and orders in force from 
time to time. 
3. 
The marks obtained by candidates and the category-wise cut-off marks for the captioned 
examination shall be displayed on the IFSCA website only after completion of the entire selection 
process and declaration of the final results of the recruitment. 
4. 
RTI applications, if any, seeking information relating to marks obtained, category-wise cut-
off marks, or allied matters shall be considered only after completion of the selection process and 
declaration of the final results of the recruitment. 
5. 
Instructions for Shortlisted Candidates 
Interview Call Letters, indicating the date, time, and venue of the interview, shall be issued 
separately to the shortlisted candidates through their registered e-mail addresses. In respect of 
candidates shortlisted for the IT Stream, details of the coding test, including the date, time, and 
venue, shall also be communicated in the Interview Call Letter. The syllabus for the coding test 
shall be uploaded separately on the IFSCA website.
