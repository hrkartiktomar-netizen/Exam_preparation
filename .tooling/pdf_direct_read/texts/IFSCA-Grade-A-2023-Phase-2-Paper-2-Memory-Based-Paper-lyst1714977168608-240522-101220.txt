Section – Banking, Capital Market, Bullion
Q.1) Explanation:
The leverage rao is a measure of a bank's capital relave to its total assets. It is calculated by dividing
the bank's Tier 1 capital by its total assets. 
The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has set a minimum leverage rao of 4% for D-SIBs in India. This means
that D-SIBs must have Tier 1 capital equal to at least 4% of their total assets. Hence, opon B is the
correct answer. 
Q.2) Explanation:
Individuals can invest in the following securies under the Retail Direct Scheme:  
• 
Government of India T-bills 
• 
Government of India dated g-secs. 
• 
State development loans (SDL) 
• 
Sovereign gold bonds (SGBs) 
To invest in these securies, individuals need to open an RDG account with the RBI. Once the account
is opened, investors can place bids for government securies in primary aucons or purchase them in
the secondary market. Hence, opon E is the correct answer.

Q.3) Explanation:
The minimum lock-in period for medium-term government deposits under the MLTGD account of the
GMS 2015 is 3 years and for long-term government deposits under the MLTGD account of the GMS
2015 is 5 years. This means that depositors cannot withdraw their deposits before the expiry from the
date of deposit. Hence, opon C is the correct answer. 
Q.4) Explanation:
Under the Gold Monezaon Scheme (GMS) in India, banks operang Medium and Long Term
Government Deposit (MLTGD) accounts are required to maintain a stock of gold equivalent to the
expected redempons by depositors in the next three months .
This spulaon ensures that there is enough physical gold to facilitate the mely redempon of
deposits made under the scheme. This helps maintain liquidity and reliability for depositors seeking to
redeem their gold deposits. Hence, opon A is the correct answer. 
Q.5) Explanation:
The following enes can parcipate in the aucon of gold under the GMS:  
• 
Reserve Bank of India (RBI): The RBI is the custodian of gold under the GMS. It aucons gold to
banks and other enes to mobilize gold into the nancial system. 
• 
Metals and Minerals Trading Corporaon of India Limited (MMTC):  MMTC is a government-
owned company that is authorized to deal in gold. It can parcipate in the aucon of gold under
the GMS to purchase gold for its own use or for resale. 
• 
Banks: Banks can parcipate in the aucon of gold under the GMS to acquire gold for their own
use or for lending to their customers. 
By allowing RBI, MMTC, and banks to parcipate in the aucon of gold, the GMS helps to mobilize gold
into the nancial system and make it more accessible to businesses and consumers. Hence, opon E
is the correct answer. 
Q.6) Explanation:
The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 is administered by the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of
India (IBBI). The RBI is responsible for administering the other four Acts listed in the opons. Hence,
opon E is the correct answer.

Q.8) Explanation:
As per the Master Circular, the RBI has withdrawn the ceiling on bank credit linked to the NOF of NBFCs
