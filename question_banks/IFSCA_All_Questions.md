### Commerce & Accounts

**Q1.** Which of the following options correctly describes the cost treatment of a by-product that does not have any realizable value?

- (a) Treated as a separate product and valued at the cost of production
- (b) Allocated to the cost of main product
- (c) Excluded from the cost of production and shown as a separate expense in the income statement
- (d) Written off as a loss in the current period
- (e) None of the above

> **Answer:** (b) Allocated to the cost of main product
>
> **Hint:** When a by-product has no realizable value, its cost cannot be independently measured and is therefore absorbed by the main product.

---

**Q2.** Subsidy provided for working capital requirements by the government will be considered as?

- (a) Capital receipt
- (b) Revenue receipt
- (c) Current Liability
- (d) Long term liability
- (e) Provisions

> **Answer:** (b) Revenue receipt
>
> **Hint:** A subsidy linked to working capital (a recurring operational need) is treated as a revenue receipt, not a capital receipt.

---

**Q4.** Which of the following assets is a non-depreciable asset?

- (a) Buildings
- (b) Land
- (c) Furniture and fixtures
- (d) Patents
- (e) Vehicles

> **Answer:** (b) Land
>
> **Hint:** Land has an indefinite useful life and is not subject to depreciation. Buildings, furniture, patents (amortisation), and vehicles are all depreciated/amortised.

---

**Q5.** Which of the following statements shows the financial position of an organisation at a specific point in time?

- (a) Income statement
- (b) Balance sheet
- (c) Cash flow statement
- (d) Statement of changes in equity
- (e) All of the above

> **Answer:** (b) Balance sheet
>
> **Hint:** The Balance Sheet is a "snapshot" of assets, liabilities, and equity at a specific date, unlike the Income Statement which covers a period.

**Q9.** Which of the following is the main objective of financial management?

- (a) Maximizing employee benefits
- (b) Minimizing production costs
- (c) Maximizing customer satisfaction
- (d) Maximizing social responsibility
- (e) Maximizing shareholder wealth

> **Answer:** (e) Maximizing shareholder wealth
>
> **Hint:** Wealth maximisation (maximising the market value of equity) is the universally accepted primary goal of financial management, superior to profit maximisation.

**Q16.** How is the working capital of an entity computed?

- (a) Excess of current liabilities over current assets
- (b) Excess of total assets over outside liabilities
- (c) Excess of current assets over current liabilities
- (d) Excess of Income over expenses
- (e) Excess of Expenses over income

> **Answer:** (c) Excess of current assets over current liabilities
>
> **Hint:** Working Capital = Current Assets − Current Liabilities. A positive figure indicates net current assets.

**Q21.** Which variable is compared to working capital when calculating the working capital turnover ratio?

**Q25.** In case of large fluctuations in purchase price of inventory, which method for calculation of cost of inventory should be used?

**Q37.** What does the ratio obtained by dividing a company's net income by its number of shares outstanding signify?

**Q43.** As per which accounting concept should an asset be valued at 'Lower of Net Realizable Value' or 'Fair Value'?

- (e) Sunk cost

> **Answer:** (c) Marginal cost
>
> **Hint:** Among the given options, opportunity cost is most closely associated with marginal cost in decision-making contexts, as it represents the value of the next-best alternative foregone at the margin.

**Q8.** A boat can travel 90 km upstream and 120 km downstream in 12 hours 30 minutes. What is the speed of the stream if the boat can travel 60 km downstream in 2 hours 30 minutes?

- (A) 5 kmph (B) 6 kmph (C) 8 kmph (D) 10 kmph (E) None of these

> **Answer:** (B) 6 kmph
>
> **Hint:** Downstream speed = 60/2.5 = 24 kmph. Time for 120 km downstream = 5 hrs. Time for 90 km upstream = 7.5 hrs → upstream speed = 12 kmph. Stream speed = (24 − 12)/2 = 6 kmph.

> **Answer:** (B) 44 years
