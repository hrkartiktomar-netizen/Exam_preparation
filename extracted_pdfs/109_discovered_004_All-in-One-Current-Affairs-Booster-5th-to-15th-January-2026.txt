SOCIAL SECTORS - HEALTH  
2.1. National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS ) 
Why in News 
• India has achieved a historic milestone by crossing 50,000 National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS) 
certifications for public health facilities . 
Key Highlights 
• Milestone Achieved:  A total of 50,373 public health facilities across all States and Union Territories have 
been certified. 
• Future Goal:  The government has set an interim target to achieve NQAS certification for at least 50% of 
all public healthcare facilities  by March 2026. 
• Key Enabler: The introduction of Virtual Assessments played a crucial role in rapidly expanding the quality 
coverage across the country.  
About National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS) 
• Established By: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) . 
• Launch Year:  2015. 
• Feature - The initiative aligns with the National Health Policy 2017  which emphasizes providing quality, 
affordable healthcare without financial hardship (Universal Health Coverage).

2.3. Prerana Programme 
Why in News 
• The Ministry of Education  announced that the Prerana Programme has successfully completed its first 
cycle. 
Key Highlights 
• Selection for Next Cycle:  To facilitate student selection for the second cycle, Prerana Utsav  is being 
organized across schools in all States and Union Territories.  
• Participants: Each weekly batch consists of 20 students (one boy and one girl from 10 different districts 
across 10 States/UTs), ensuring gender balance and diversity.  
• Curriculum: Delivered by experts from institutions like IIT Gandhinagar, IIM Ahmedabad, and the Indian 
Knowledge Systems (IKS) Division . 
• Core Values of Programme: The curriculum is based on nine core values: 
o Swabhiman aur Vinay (Dignity and Humility)  
o Shaurya aur Sahas (Valour and Courage)  
o Parishram aur Samarpan (Hard Work and Dedication)  
o Satyanishtha aur Shuchita (Integrity and Purity)  
o Karuna aur Sewa (Compassion and Service)  
o Navachar aur Jigyasa (Innovation and Curiosity)  
o Vividhta aur Ekta (Diversity and Unity)  
o Shraddha aur Vishwas (Faith and Trust)  
o Swatantrata aur Kartavya (Freedom and Duty)  
About Prerana Programme 
o Nodal Ministry: Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of Education . 
o Type: A week-long residential experiential learning program . 
o Location: Conducted at the historic Vernacular School in Vadnagar, Gujarat (established in 1888). 
The site hosts the Prerana Sankul.

CET1 ratios to qualify for the maximum payout compared to other banks.  
Basel III norms  govern the dividend

banks to conserve capital for long -
term resilience.

• Launch: September 2024 (announced in Union Budget 2024 -25)

• GOBAR-Dhan projects models - 4 
o Individual Household (HH)  
▪ Adopted by households having 3 or more cattle  
▪ Biogas is generated from plants used for cooking  
▪ Slurry generated used as manure  
o Community 
▪ Biogas plants are constructed for minimum number of households - 5 to 10 
▪ Operated and managed by Gram Panchayats/Self -Help Groups (SHGs)  
▪ Gas generated supplied to households restaurants /institutions  
▪ Slurry used by community as organic manure in agriculture or sold to farmers  
o Clusters 
▪ Individual biogas plants installed in number of households in a village/ group of villages  
▪ Biogas used by households  
▪ Slurry collected at a common place, separated into solid and liquid parts and then fortified 
and sold as bio-fertilizers 
o Commercial Compressed Biogas (CBG)  
▪ Set up by Entrepreneurs / Cooperative Societies/ Gaushalas etc.  
▪ Raw biogas produced is compressed and can be used as vehicular fuel and / or sold to 
industries 
▪ Slurry generated converted into organic manure / bio -fertilizer and can be sold to farmers  
▪ These plants are now eligible for Central Financial Assistance (CFA) from the Ministry of 
New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) up to ₹4 crore per project.  
• Over 750+ Biogas/CBG plants are functional across India, covering nearly all states/UTs.  
• A mandatory blending of Compressed Biogas (CBG) in the CNG (Transport) and PNG (Domestic) segments 
has been introduced, starting at 1% in 2025 -26 and increasing to 5% by 2028 -29. 
• The Government provides ₹1,500 per metric tonne on Fermented Organic Manure (FOM) and Liquid

Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS) Scheme  
• Launch/Background formerly known as Shramik Vidyapeeth, was being implemented through NGOs in 
country since 1967 and renamed as Jan Shikshan Sansthan in 2000  
• Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship  
• Aim: To provide skill training in a non -formal mode at doorsteps of beneficiary through registered 
Societies (NGOs) with 100% grant from Government of India  
• Objective: To increase household income by promoting self/wage employment through skill development 
training 
• Tenure: Till 31st March 2026 
• Target 
o To train 28.18 lakh beneficiaries from FY 2022 -23 to FY 2025 -26 
o To expand in aspirational, LWE, tribal, border and other backward districts of country  
▪ to increase annual training capacity from 4.76 lakh beneficiaries in FY 2021 -22 to 9.07 
lakh beneficiaries in the FY 2025 -26
